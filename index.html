The Venue

A purpose-built complex on Main Defence Road

Set near Gate #3 of DHA Rahbar Phase 2, Lords Event Complex brings five separate halls together in one walled complex — so a grand barat and an intimate family function can each have a space that fits.

  • Five halls under one roof — pick the hall that matches your guest count, from family functions to full-scale weddings.
  • Room for 2,500+ guests across a 40-kanal complex on Main Defence Road.
  • Easy to reach — near Gate #3, Phase XI Block N, DHA Rahbar Phase 2, serving Defence, Valencia, Wapda Town and the wider south Lahore side.
  • Rated 4.3 out of 5 by 399 reviewers on Shadiyana, the wedding-venue platform.
